Day-wise Curriculum Breakdown

Daily 5:00 PM – 6:30 PM IST (90 mins total per day)
DAY 1 · Mon, 26 Oct

Intro & Standards

Module 1 (50m): CIA triad, threat landscape, career paths & case study.

Module 2 (40m): ISO/IEC 27001 & 27002, NIST CSF 2.0, PCI-DSS, SOC 2, GDPR, HIPAA.

DAY 2 · Tue, 27 Oct

Governance & Crypto

Module 3 (40m): GRC overview, risk registers, policy hierarchy, incident response.

Module 4 (50m): Symmetric & asymmetric encryption, hashing, PKI, TLS/HTTPS.

DAY 3 · Wed, 28 Oct

System Security

Module 5 (90m): OS hardening, endpoint protection, patch & vulnerability management, firewalls, VPNs, segmentation, access control (RBAC, least privilege).

DAY 4 · Thu, 29 Oct

Web App Security

Module 6 (90m): OWASP Top 10, secure session management, auth/authorization flaws, API security, secure SDLC and DevSecOps practices.

DAY 5 · Fri, 30 Oct

Threats & Tools

Module 7 (40m): Global threat tracking (CVE, CVSS, NVD, CERT/CISA advisories).

Module 8 (50m): Hands-on tools (Nmap, Wireshark, scanners, Metasploit, SIEM).

DAY 6 · Sat, 31 Oct

Hacking & Solutions

Module 9 (50m): Ethical hacking methodology, pentest vs red teaming, bug bounty.

Module 10 (40m): Industrial security architecture, vendor tools, OT/ICS, build vs buy.
