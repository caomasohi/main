About Our Organization

Bridging Industry & Academia to Shape Future-Ready Talent

MASOHI PRIVATE LIMITED operates at the critical intersection of Education, Employability, Skill Development, Training, Internship Enablement, and Industry-Academia Collaboration.

Who We Are

A Dedicated Workforce Development Company

MASOHI addresses one of the defining challenges of modern higher education: the gap between academic curricula and actual industry workplace requirements.

India produces a vast number of talented engineering and university graduates every year. While institutions provide essential theoretical rigor, modern technology employers require practical problem-solving capabilities, cloud tools, code collaboration, and verified project experience.

MASOHI provides the structured bridge — delivering industry-aligned training, expert practitioner mentorship, guided internship enablement, and continuous faculty development.
