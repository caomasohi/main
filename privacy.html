Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 | MASOHI PRIVATE LIMITED

1. Overview & Commitment

MASOHI PRIVATE LIMITED ("MASOHI", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and integrity of personal information collected through our corporate website, programme registration forms, and institutional interfaces.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information directly submitted by users through voluntary forms or inquiries, including:

  • Contact Details: Full name, email address, phone/mobile number.
  • Institutional & Profile Information: College/university affiliation, department, company name, designation, and user type (Student, Faculty, Institution, Industry).
  • Inquiry Details: Specific programme interests, questions, and collaboration scope.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used exclusively for legitimate business and academic purposes:

  • Responding to inquiries, FDP requests, and partnership proposals.
  • Transmitting relevant course outlines, schedules, and event access details.
  • Improving our educational offerings and platform performance.
  • Complying with applicable legal and statutory requirements.

4. Data Protection & Non-Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third-party marketing entities. Access to personal information is restricted to authorized personnel who need to know that information to process your request.

5. Contact for Privacy Inquiries

For questions regarding this policy or to request updates to your submitted information, please reach out to our privacy desk at:
[Privacy Officer: contact@masohi.in]
